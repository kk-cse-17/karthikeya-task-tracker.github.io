📖 How to get your free Firebase URL (100% Free, 2 mins)
  1. Go to console.firebase.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click "Add project" → name it karthikeya-tracker → click Create.
  3. On the left menu, click Build → Realtime Database → click "Create Database".
  4. Choose location, click Next, select "Start in test mode" (allows read & write), and click Enable.
  5. Copy the URL displayed at the top (e.g. https://karthikeya-tracker-default-rtdb.firebaseio.com/) and paste it above!
